The 6S Method

Not one magic device.
Six layers of defense.

Myopia isn't caused by one thing, and it can't be stopped by one thing. Six evidence-based modules, combined into one personalized plan.

S1
Healthy Light Environment
The cheapest, fastest win in myopia prevention — turn an eye-straining home into an eye-friendly one.
S2
Red Light Therapy
Minutes a day of low-level red light, shown in studies to slow axial elongation.
S3
Optical Correction
Defocus lenses and ortho-k that actively manage growth, not just correct it.
S4
Near-to-Far Viewing
Turn close-up work into distance viewing — homework without the strain.
S5
Vision Function Training
A fitness coach for the eyes: accommodation and vergence, trained.
S6
Nutrition & Medical Support
Lutein, low-dose atropine and other medical options — always with your eye care professional.
